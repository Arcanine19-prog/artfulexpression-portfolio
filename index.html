About the artist

Prasenjit Choudhury

Paints and sketches under the name artfulexpression — portraits lit like they're caught mid-thought, palette-knife landscapes, and the odd fan piece for good measure. Based in Agartala, Tripura, and currently studying Computer Science at VIT Chennai, he works across oil, acrylic, graphite and pen — acrylic is the medium of choice. The long-term goal: illustrating for Marvel.

How a commission works

01

Browse & pick

Look through the gallery and note down a piece, a style, or a photo you'd like turned into art.

02

Send a message

Email or DM with the medium, size and timeline you have in mind — a quick back-and-forth from there.

03

Confirm & create

Price and delivery get agreed before anything is painted, so there are no surprises either side.

Start a conversation
